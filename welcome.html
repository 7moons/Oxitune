Welcome to Oxitune

Welcome to Oxitune

YouTube Music on your desktop, with media keys, synced lyrics, an equalizer, Listen Together and more. A few choices first; you can change all of them later in Settings.

  • Sign in afterwards YouTube Music opens when you finish here. You sign in on Google's own page: Oxitune's code runs only on music.youtube.com and never sees your password.
  • Your account, your library It is the real YouTube Music: your playlists, likes and recommendations, exactly as on the web.

On your desktop

How Oxitune fits in next to your other apps.

Show on Discord Shows what you're listening to in your Discord status
Start with the system Opens Oxitune in the tray when you sign in
Keep playing when closed Closing the window hides it to the system tray
Song notifications Shows a desktop notification when the song changes
Pause when the screen locks Minimizing the window doesn't pause playback

Lyrics and sound

The equalizer, crossfade and speed are in Settings whenever you want them.

Timed lyrics Adds lyrics that follow the song to the Lyrics tab
Open the Lyrics tab by itself Switches to the Lyrics tab when a song that has lyrics starts
Ambient mode Tints the app with colors from the current song's artwork
Normalize volume Evens out loudness between songs

What goes online

Besides YouTube Music itself, Oxitune connects only for these:

  • Lyrics The song's title, artist, length and video id go to the lyrics providers you have on (Settings → Add-ons)
  • Listen Together Only while you are in a session, end-to-end encrypted, through public brokers that see neither the songs nor the people
  • Updates and plugins A check for new signed versions and plugin updates, on GitHub
Translate lyrics Shows a translation under each line. The lyrics are sent to Google Translate
Romanize lyrics Shows lyrics written in other scripts (Japanese, Korean, Cyrillic…) in Latin letters too. The lyrics are sent to Google Translate
Phone remote Control playback from a phone on the same Wi-Fi. Takes effect after restarting the app.

You're all set

YouTube Music opens next. A few tips there show where Oxitune's features live.

  • The Oxitune menu The round button in YouTube Music's top bar: Settings and the equalizer, the mini player, local music, Listen Together and the phone remote
  • Settings anytime Ctrl+, or F2 in YouTube Music, or the tray menu
